## Description ![[Archerfish.png|A close up of the Archer fish in game.]]
The Archer fish is one of the many fish that can be obtained , it is
characterized by it's zebra pattern and small body.

Upon activation the Archer fish starts Propelling water from it's oral
cavity(it's weaker than the rocket so it's basically just a small
thruster).
