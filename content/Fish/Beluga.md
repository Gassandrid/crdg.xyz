## Description ![[Belgua_whale.png|A close up of the Beluga in game.]] The
Beluga is one of the many fish that can be obtained, it is characterized
by it's white dolphin shape and big mind.

## Function

Upon activation it send's out a blue signal wave (which can be glued if
you time it right) that activates/deactivates anything that it hits.
