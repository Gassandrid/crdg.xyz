## Description ![[BaskingShark.png|A close up of the Basking shark in game.]] The
Basking shark is one of the many Sharks that can be obtained, it is
characterized by it's Big mouth and leathery colour.

## Function

Upon activation the Basking shark will start sucking in things for 5 sec
and then it will stop sucking in things.
