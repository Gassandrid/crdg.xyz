![[wiki-1791958868056-45b31963-gold.png|300]] [[Gold|Gold]] can be found at
the [[Map/Gas-Station|Gas Station]]. It is used to
make golden lemonade to obtain the [[Badges/Delicious-Citrus |Delicious Citrus]] badge.
It is in a secret room above the shop area of the
Petrol Station.

## How to extract gold
- A good way to get the gold out is being gluing it to a trigger, holding the trigger and then while the the trigger is being held walk into a seat with a missing top. This will cause you to stick out of the wall letting you jump to exit the secret room with the gold and trigger with you.

- Another good way to get gold out is either slamming it against the wall, or
attaching two ropes, glitch one inside and glue it to gold, spike the
one outside and shrink it over and over (button on the BLUE side) and it
will just pop out.

## Uses
### To obtain the [[Badges/Delicious-Citrus|Delicious Citrus]] badge.
##### ==Getting the badge==
To get the [[Badges/Delicious-Citrus|Delicious Citrus]] badge, you must first extract the gold bar. After extracting the gold bar transport it to the [[Map/Cathedral|Cathedral]] and locate the furnace room, after creating [[Components/Regular-Components/Planter|Planters]] melt the gold bar in the furnace and place a planter under the furnace to pour gold into it. Once the planter has turned golden, move the next planter into the same position to turn it golden too, it is recommended to get 3-5 golden planters. Plant a ==Lemon== into the golden planter and wait for it to grow, this planter will only make one ==golden lemon== because a normal lemon was planted. After getting your golden lemon plant it into another golden planter and wait for it to grow more golden lemons, plant those golden lemons until you all your remaining golden planters are golden lemon trees. Extract only your golden lemons and obtain the ==Knife== and then cut the golden lemons until you have **Half Slices**. Crush or Juice the half slices using [[Components/Regular-Components/Linear-Actuator|Linear Actuators]] and collect the droplets in a [[Items/Regular-Items/Glass|Glass Cup]] until it is full and then drink the glass. A red message should appear and then you should get the [[Badges/Delicious-Citrus|Delicious Citrus]] badge.
### To get golden planters
Gold can be used to obtain golden planters.
#### Uses of golden planters
They make things grow faster.
