The **LED Light** is a **Component** obtainable through the [[items/regular-items/component-machine|Component Machine]].

## Function

The LED Light is used to make different color lights, depending on
the color of the activation source. 

For example, if the LED Light is activated with a [[components/Button|Button]], The LED Light will generate a light that is the same color as the Button. If the LED Light was activated with a [[components/Tripwire|Tripwire]], the LED Light will be the color of the Tripwire. This is the case for any activation source.

## Additional Information

These can be useful for making signs and text. Activating the LED Light with a pure
black Button will make the light rainbow.
