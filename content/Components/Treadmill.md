The **Treadmill** is a **Component** obtainable through the [[items/regular-items/component-machine|Component Machine]]. It resembles a rectangular portion of a conveyor belt.

## Function 

When the Treadmill is sent a signal or the button on the side of the Treadmill is clicked, The Treadmill's conveyor portion will begin spinning in the direction of the arrow. Multiple Treadmills can be used together to create large sections of conveyor belt.
