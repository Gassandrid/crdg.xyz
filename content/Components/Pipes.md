## Overview

Pipes are components primarily available with component machine. They allow for transporting liquids and gases from one point to another.

Pipes can also transfer components spawned by component factory component\*Straight pipeIt is a straight pipe, approximately 2.2 studs long, can be extended or shortened on anvil by combining with either another straight pipe or a gun.Liquid/gas will come out of opposite side if possible.

Available in component machine.
Turn pipe
Acts the same as a straight pipe, however features a 90 degree turn.

Available in component machine.Valve pipeActs the same as a straight pipe, however features a turn valve, that can be activated with a click. When the said valve is facing parallel to the pipe, liquids/gases will pass through, otherwise not.
Valve pipe can also be activated with a regular signal going to the pipe, this also applies to all other pipes that feature a valve\*

Available in component machine.Turn valve pipeIt is a T shaped pipe, practically features both a turn pipe and a straight pipe behaviors, which can be swapped between with a valve. In default state, the valve goes parallel to the longer section of the pipe. Connect either of that long part ends' to your pipework, and the liquid will pass on the opposite end. If valve is enabled, the liquid will go instead to the side, doing a 90 degree turn.

Available in component machine.Split pipeA Y shaped pipe, allows to either split the pipe into 2 pipes or combine 2 into 1.
Usually gases will go out of the end which is positioned higher than the other, and liquids will go out of the one which is positioned lower than the other, with some exceptions present. Otherwise the either of ends is chosen randomly\*

Available in component machine.Quad pipeAn X shaped pipe, that also features a 5th input/output in its center. Acts similarly to split pipe, just more outputs.

Available in component machine.Signal pipeA straight pipe with a socket attached. Acts the same as straight pipe, however will send a signal through it's socket when a liquid passes through.

Socket can occasionally not send the signal if the liquid flow is more than 1 unit/second\*

Available in component machine.Two way valve pipeAn X shaped pipe, with a valve in the middle. Allows 2 of the opposite inputs/outputs to transfer liquid/gas between each other if the valve is facing parallel with them. The other 2 ends do not work that way.

Available in component machine.

## Rotating pipe

### A straight pipe with a ball bearing in the middle, acts the same as straight pipe, but can rotate around itself.

### Crafted on an anvil with a straight pipe and a ball bearing.

## Hose

### A pipe that has 2 solid ends and a Roblox rope constraint connecting these 2 ends. Allows for the most easiest connection between 2 ends of pipes with great flexibility, or allows to connect 2 ends if there is a solid wall between them.

Available for purchase from Blimp for 1-2 cups of honey.

## Funnel pipe

### A large pipe with wide square hole on one end and a regular round pipe on the other. Automatically collects fresh water during the rain, if is not obstructed by a roof. Allows for easier collection of liquids on the floor or in cases when player has to literally catch liquids from somewhere.

Available for purchase from the Blimp for 2-3 cups of honey.
