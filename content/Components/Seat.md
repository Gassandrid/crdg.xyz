The **Seat** is a **Component** obtainable through the [[items/regular-items/component-machine|Component Machine]]. It resembles a yellow seat with a backrest.

## Function

The Seat allows players to sit down. It can be glued to carts and player-made machines. Can be used in conjunction with an [[components/input-receiver|Input Receiver]] to make interactable machines, such as a vehicle.
