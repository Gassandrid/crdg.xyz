The **Uranium Rod** is a **Component** obtainable through the [[items/regular-items/component-machine|Component Machine]]. It is also used as an ingredient in the [[collectibles/cauldron|Cauldron]] and a crafting ingredient in the [[features/anvil|Anvil]]. Visually, the Uranium Rod is a neon green glowing rod.

## Function

The Uranium Rod has no intrinsic function; instead, it has a number of supplementary uses.

For example, the Uranium Rod is used to fuel the [[components/Fab|Fab]] and the [[components/nuclear-motor|Nuclear Motor]]. It is also used in a number of Cauldron and Anvil recipes, viewable on their respective articles.
