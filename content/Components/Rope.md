The **Rope** is a **Component** obtainable through the [[items/regular-items/component-machine|Component Machine]]. The Rope has a red and blue end which are attached by a brown rope.

## Function

The Rope is used for connecting objects together. When the red part of the Rope is activated, the Rope will extend slightly in length, up to approximately 30 studs. When the blue part is activated, the Rope will shorten slightly in length.

## Additional Information

- In cases where a stable, fixed rope is needed, the [[components/cable|Cable]] may be a better option than the Rope, as unlike the Rope the Cable will not stretch when under heavy load.
- The Rope does not transfer signals between each end, unlike the Cable.
