The golden lemon is, as the name implies, a [[Components/Lemon|lemon]] with a [[Components/Gold|gold]] outside. It can be made by planting a [[Components/Lemon|lemon]] in a golden [[components/planter|planter]], in which at least 1 golden lemon will grow. After obtaining one, you can replant it to get more. It can be used to get the [[badges/delicious-citrus|Delicious Citrus]] badge.
