The **Water Heater**, also known as the **Boiler Hybrid** is a notable piping component added on **October 23rd, 2025**. It spawns inside of the gas station back room. It is used to turn liquids into other ones as well as producing gas linked to that liquid. It can also [[Cauldron#Potion Concentrating|concentrate potions]].

## Usage

Turning on the water heater will rise up the water temperature inside of it in a span of around 10 seconds, indicated by the "Water Temp" position indicator. Once it reaches 120°C, the water heater is now functional and it can start heating up liquids.
