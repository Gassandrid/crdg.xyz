The **Cork** is a **Component** obtainable through the [[items/regular-items/component-machine|Component Machine]]. It resembles a cork stopper.

## Function

The Cork's sole function is to plug outlets on [[components/pipes|Pipes]]. When the Cork touches a pipe outlet, it will snap into it and prevent any fluid from leaving through that outlet. To remove the Cork, simply grab it and it will pop out of the Pipe.
