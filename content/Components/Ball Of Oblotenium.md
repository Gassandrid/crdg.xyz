The **Ball of Oblotenium** is a **Regular Component** added on **August 28th, 2022**. Visually, it resembles a small ball of hard rock. It is used to add additional weight to contraptions to change them physically. It is also used as an ingredient in the [[Cauldron]].

> [!Tip] Tip
> **Balls of Oblotenium** can be used to add weight to [[Anti-Gravity Device|Anti-Gravity Devices]] to lower their gravity much further. You can use this to your advantage to make flying much easier.

## Additional information

The ball of oblotenium is a component in CRDG. It is a small metal
sphere.

## Function

The ball of oblotenium has no special properties other than it's
weight. It is very dense and is hard to lift, if multiple are glued
together a player can't get it off the ground. They are primarily used
when a lot of extra weight is needed, such as anchoring batteries at the
cart dealership, however [[Spikes|Spikes]] are better
for this purpose. They make a great counterweight if you need something
to stay upright or faced a certain way, especially if it is suspended
via ropes or other methods.
