The **Ball of Oblotenium** is a **Component** obtainable through the [[items/regular-items/component-machine|Component Machine]]. Visually, it resembles a small ball of hard rock. It is also used as an ingredient in the [[Cauldron]].

## Function

The Ball of Oblotenium has no special properties other than it's
weight. As such, it works well as a counterweight for machines and was also used as a makeshift anchor before the additon of [[Spikes|Spikes]]. They are useful in builds that require something
to stay upright or faced a certain way, especially if it is suspended
via ropes or other methods.

> [!Tip] Tip
> **Balls of Oblotenium** can be used to add weight to [[Anti-Gravity Device|Anti-Gravity Devices]] to lower their gravity much further. You can use this to your advantage to make flying much easier.
