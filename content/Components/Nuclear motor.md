The **Nuclear Motor** is a **Component** obtainable through the [[items/regular-items/component-machine|Component Machine]]. Visually,
it is a small metal machine with various pipes and struts attached to it.

## Function

The Nuclear Motor is inert until a [[components/Uranium-Rod|Uranium Rod]] is inserted into its fuel compartment. Once inserted, the front plate of the Nuclear Motor will slowly begin spinning and it will start producing a chugging noise. 

The longer the Uranium Rod is in the motor, the faster it will spin. Within 15-20 seconds of the process the motor will begin spinning at incredibly fast speeds, causing the entire Nuclear Motor to shake violently if not anchored down.

When the Uranium Rod has been in the Nuclear Motor for approximately 45 seconds, the Nuclear Motor will begin to glow orange and explode violently shortly thereafter. The entire process takes approximately **50 seconds** from Uranium Rod insertion to explosion.

Unlike other explosives, the Nuclear Motor releases several separate
explosions in quick succession when it is triggered.

## Additional Information

After inserting the Uranium Rod into the Nuclear Motor, you should immediately staple or glue the rod in place so that it does not fall out of the fuel compartment.

> [!Tip] Tip
> If the Uranium Rod is destroyed while in the fuel compartment of the Nuclear Motor, such as by being shot with a [[components/gun|Gun]], the Nuclear Motor will "stabilize" and continue spinning at a constant, fixed speed (the speed varies depending on how long the Uranium Rod has been inserted for). This can be extremely useful for creating machines that require constant circular rotation.
