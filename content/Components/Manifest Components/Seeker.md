The **Seeker** is a [[content/Features/Manifests|Manifests]] item that can be acquired through **Fishing** or by planting seeds in the [[Planter]] and waiting 19 or 24 hours. It is a sort of a contained version of the floating eye from gas.

---

## Usage

When spawned, the **Seeker** immediately locks onto the nearest player. It has a long tracking range and also has an output on its back. For example, if a [[1911 Pistol]] is attached to the output, the **Seeker** will continuously fire at its target until the player its looking at is dead. Builders commonly use **Seekers** as turrets or even as makeshift gyro.

## Description

The Seeker, also known as the Eye or the Flying Eye, is a flying object.
The Seeker begins to levitate and chase the player if they attempt to
pick it up. The Seeker will not stop chasing the victim until they die,
and has collisions. It is practically impossible to operate carts with
it, as it will do it's best to stay directly infront of the player and
can easily knock carts off the rails.

## Location

The Seeker is placed behind the [[Gas Station|Gas Station]]. The Seeker is guarded by
the [[knife trap|knife trap]]. The trap is rather
easy to avoid if you are careful, so it isn't much of an obstacle.
