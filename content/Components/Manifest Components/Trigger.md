The **Trigger** was a **component** that could be created using the component machine. However, the trigger was removed from the [[Gamepasses/component-machine|Component Machine]] 2 hours after its first addition in an update called "**NO FUN ALLOWED**" and made a [[Features/Manifests|Manifest]] due to how glitchy it was. Then, even after it became a ==manifest== it was briefly removed until it was readded to the [[Gamepasses/component-machine|Component Machine]] 2 years after its original removal from it during the ==Aerodynamics Update==.

---

## Usage
#### 1. To create custom items.
The **Trigger** can be held and if there is anything glued to the trigger, it will be held with it. When the trigger is held it will act as an Item in your inventory. However, whenever the trigger is unequipped it instantly drops to the ground reducing its usefulness. When you ==Left Click== while holding a trigger it will create a signal activating anything glued to it as long as a [[Components/Regular-Components/Rubber-slab|Rubber slab]] or rubberized glue isn't used to connect it to the trigger.
#### 2. To extract secret items.
The **Trigger** is incredibly useful for extracting things that are normally only taken out using glitches such as the [[Components/Miscellaneous/Gold|Gold bar]] and the ==Verity Box== at the [[Map/Gas-Station|Gas Station]]. This is because when the trigger is picked up it and anything glued to it is instantly teleported to your hand.
#### 3. Transportation.
The **Trigger** can be used to **transport** things easily around the map. This can include things that are usually heavy such as the [[Components/Regular-Components/Ball-Of-Oblotenium|Ball of Oblotenium]] or things like entire structures.
#### 4. Flying
The **Trigger** can be used to easily go from [[Map/Spawn|Spawn]] into the [[Map/Cathedral|Cathedral]] by gluing a [[Components/Regular-Components/Balloon|Balloon]] with its anchor destroyed using a [[Components/Regular-Components/Gun|Gun]] to the trigger and then holding the trigger while standing near an ==Air Compressor== to fill up the balloon. When the balloon is filled enough, you can jump to then start floating upwards.
> #### ⚠️ You **CANNOT** transport things using the trigger method as the balloon will only keep on floating upwards even when the trigger is unequipped.

- **Note;** you will ==**not**== have collision with anything glued to the trigger since ==Thursday, October 1st 2026==
