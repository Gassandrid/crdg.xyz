The **Trigger** was once a [[features/Manifests|Manifests]] item that could be acquired through [[fish/fishing|Fishing]] or planting [[components/seed|Seeds]] in a public server and waiting 24 hours. However, it has since been removed due to a lot of glitches with it, and then readded in the Aeronautics update, and can now be spawned via the [component machine](https://crdg.xyz/gamepasses/component-machine).

## Function

The **Trigger** is used  to make your own items, being able to glue guns, components, or custom builds onto the **Trigger**.

When picked up and clicked, the trigger will send out a signal to anything it is glued to.
