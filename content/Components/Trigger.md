The **Trigger** was once a [[features/Manifests|Manifests]] item that could be acquired through [[fish/fishing|Fishing]] or planting [[components/seed|Seeds]] in a public server and waiting 24 hours. However, it has since been removed due to a lot of glitches with it, and then readded in the Aeronautics update.

## Function

The \*\*Trigger\*\* is used  to make your own items, being able to add guns, components, or custom builds onto the \*\*Trigger\*\*.
The trigger now has more uses from when it was removed, as it can be used to fly, shoot people to death, or fling yourself.
