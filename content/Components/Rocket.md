## Usage

When activated, the **Rocket** propels itself forward in the direction it is facing. Before the [[content/Features/Manifests|Manifests]] update, it was spawnable. Players often use them to travel quickly across the map, build flying machines, or to provide an extra boost to their vehicle.

## Additional information

The rocket is a [[Manifests|manifest]] component
obtainable only in public servers. It is a large, dark gray tube with a
button on the front.

# Function

When the button on the front of the rocket is pressed or the rocket is
powered, the rocket provides extreme thrust in the direction it is
facing. The front of the rocket is where the button is, not the hole.

It can be used to make crude flying machines, especially with the alien
gyro, although it is unstable and hard to control unless used properly.
As a manifest, it can be obtained from fishing, growing
[[Seed|seeds]], etc. and can be stored in the manifest
menu.
