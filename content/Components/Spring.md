The **Spring** is a **Component** obtainable through the [[items/regular-items/component-machine|Component Machine]]. It resembles a coil spring with two red ends.

## Function

The Spring is highly elastic, being able to snap back into position easily when not under load. When activated, the Spring will quickly toggle between a shortened position and an extended position. This can exert a lot of force at once, which could be useful for a multitude of builds.
