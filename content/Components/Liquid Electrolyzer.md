The **Liquid Electrolyzer** is a Piping-based component added on September 11th, 2026 in the Aerodynamics Update. When powered on, it can separate a liquid into either a gas coming from the red side or another liquid coming from the blue side, or both. 
## Usage

The electrolyzer is necessary in [[PVC Board|PVC]] production, as it is the only component to be able to produce [[Chlorine]].
