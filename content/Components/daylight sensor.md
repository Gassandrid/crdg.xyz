The **daylight sensor** is a **Manifest Component** added on **July 14th, 2026**. Visually, it resembles a customized [[XOR Gate]] with a solar panel on top of it as well as yellow and blue inputs.

## Usage

Sending a signal to the yellow input will switch the time of day assigned to the **daylight sensor**, toggling between day and night, indicated by the icon on top of the logic gate. Sending a signal to the blue input will attempt to turn on the logic gate depending on if the correct time was set or not. If the logic gate is correctly turned on, signal will be able to go through the grey part.
