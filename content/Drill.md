A drill is a component from the component machine. It can be used to get infinite fluid from the ground depending on where the drill is placed.

| Drill Location | Fluid |
| --- | --- |
| Spawn | Still water |
| Bill's Batteries | Crude oil |
| Cart Dealership | Crude oil |
| Gas Station | Diesel |
| Cathedral | Crude oil |
