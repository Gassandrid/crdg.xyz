The **Dedicated Realist** badge used to be obtained by taking the robux from [[Bill's batteries]] and transporting it to the vending machine at the [[Gas Station]].

This badge is no longer obtainable, but those who got it while it was obtainable got the [[Teddy Bear]] item.

It has a 20% chance to explode when you hug it. You can hug it by clicking anywhere.
