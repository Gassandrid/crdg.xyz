Gives player Miracle of momentum, which is also known as "leap". Player can use leap by pressing G, as shown in the icon of said gamepass. 

> [!Info] 
> This game pass is only avaliable during black friday sale! Active from November 29 till December 3rd

---
### Usage

To activate the ability, player must press g (on pc), press on special button (on mobile) or hold both joystics at the same time (on console). This will fling the character forward.

>![[LeapExample.gif|300x300]]
>Player using miracle of momentum

> [!Tip]
> You can shoot makarov behind yourself in mid-fling to greatly boost yourself and vice versa. This can be used to skip major parts of the game!

## Additional information

![[MiracleOfMomentum.png|156x156px\|alt=The icon for the Miracle of momentum.\|Icon]] ![[DashGif.gif|A gif of a user using the miracle of momentum.]] The miracle of momentum
is a miracle that allows the user to dash forward. It has been up for
sale on both [[Black Friday|Black Fridays]],
with the first time costing 100 Robux and the second time costing 200
Robux. It is expected to return in the next [[Black Friday|Black Friday]] event.

### Function

By pressing G, the user will be flung forward about 80 studs, with a
cooldown of approximately 8 seconds. It flings in the direction that the
player character is facing, not the direction of the camera. Momentum
does not work when the player is
[[Destabilization system|destabilized]], it
also destabilizes the player on use.

When used with the [[Makarov|Makarov]], the miracle
of momentum's fling can be made much stronger (albeit less accurate)
