## Overview

The Miracle of Sledging is a Limited Black Friday Miracle costing 1600 Robux and is only avaible on Nov 27th 2026-Dec 3rd 2026. It gives the player the "Thundersledge" item which has 3 uses: LMB causes a simple swing, E causes a shove that unglues parts, and F causes you to slam the hammer into the ground, launching yourself upwards, and upon landing create a shockwave that damages players.
