**Bee repellant** makes [[Bees]] ignore you. Making [[Beehive]] obtainment harmless. However, it only applies to you. Any player that doesn't own the gamepass will still be attacked by [[Bees]]

> [!info] This still doesn't prevent bees from doing DAMAGE to you!
> If you stand inside the bees when you break the bee hive, they can still damage you, but they won't actively chase you.
