## Information

The Anvil is a feature added to CRDG on the 24th of February, 2026, announced on a Tuesday at 6:33 AM UTC. The Anvil appears as a large, traditional single-horn anvil resembling the London pattern, with a tapered horn, rectangular face and heel, and a broad base. A hammer is attached to it by a blue rope.

The Anvil allows players to combine specific sets of components and other items through recipes, producing new and often more complex components, or variations of the same component. These recipes can result in useful tools and devices, while others are primarily intended for experimentation, novelty, or entertainment.

---

## Functionality

To use the Anvil, the required components or items for a recipe must first be placed on top of it. The player can then strike the Anvil or the items resting on it, causing any valid combination to be consumed and replaced by the item produced by that recipe.

When struck, the Anvil produces a metallic hammering sound and briefly releases sparks, providing visual and audible feedback for the interaction.
