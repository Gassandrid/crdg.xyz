A taste is a property of certain objects which causes them to have a
message appear at the top of the screen when licked or drank. A glass can be used to make it easier to drink stuff but with enough skill you can drip it into your mouth from any source. There are also smells from gasses which give you a message just like tastes.

😡= Tasting or smelling this will damage you by about 10 HP

☺️= Doesn't damage you even though it sounds like it should
