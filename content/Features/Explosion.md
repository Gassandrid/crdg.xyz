Explosions are effects that appear when certain actions are performed,
such as a [[Jerry can|Jerry can]] hitting the
ground hard enough. Explosions usually unweld parts and damage players.
They are all roughly the same size.

Explosions can occur when:

- A [[Jerry can|Jerry can]] impacts something hard
  enough, or is powered.
- A [[Stinger|Stinger]]'s missile hits something.
- A [[Teddy Bear|Teddy Bear]] is hugged.
- A [[C4|C4]] is detonated.
- [[Traps#Cannon|The Cannon]] is shot.
- [[Traps#Moving Platform|The Emergency Button]] is
  pressed.
- A [[nuclear motor|nuclear motor]] is active
  too long.
- A fuel tank (from a [[Carts|cart]]) hits something
  too hard
