**Water** is a miscellaneous-type [[Fluids|Liquid]] added on June 23rd, 2024 during the Summer Update. It is able to be produced by a [[Water Cooler]], but can also be obtained by taking the pre-filled glass of water at [[Gas Station]]. Water is also part of a [[Cauldron]] potion recipe, being inside of the Cure potion.

## Usage

Water can be used to quickly fill up glasses to transport them easier to various locations, if you do not have the [sack (marbles)](https://crdg.xyz/items/regular-items/marbles)

If used with the spray bottle, it can clear glue.
