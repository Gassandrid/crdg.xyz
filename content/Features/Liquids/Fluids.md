In CRDG, a fluid is a liquid or a gas. Liquids can be heated with a water cooler or boiled with a boiler from the gas station back room, the campfire, or a tiki torch. Boiling doesn't always make a gas because it's named after the fact that it heats enough to boil water. Gasses can be condensed with the gas condenser component.
