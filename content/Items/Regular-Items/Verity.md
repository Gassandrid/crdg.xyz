# Verity
> [!info] Title
> Help needed in finding every type of verity and monster form.

Originally, Verity is a sentient AI made by a youtuber called thatmob. The series became very famous and was later added into CRDG during the the ==Aerodynamics update== which happened on ==Saturday, September 12th 2026==.

### Location of Verity
Verity is found inside a cardboard box inside the room on the right from the shop at the [[Map/Gas-Station|Gas Station]]. The box is similar to how Verity is contained within the original thatmob verity youtube series.

### Usage

The box can be opened using the [[Items/Regular-Items/Box-cutter|Box Cutter]] on the tape part, opening the box will release verity.
Once verity is released, it will start rolling towards the player. To restrain verity, you can simply glue it to [[Components/Regular-Components/Spikes|Spikes]] or just throw it into the void.

Verity will always attempt to face the player, even when glued to objects. This can be useful for some creations.

Verity can also be angered by typing some stuff in chat which will make verity enter its monster form
## Variants
There is more than one type of verity, such as Skibity and Scoobity, all with different voices and colors. A different color of verity has different behaviour.
#### Variants of verity
| Name | Description | Behaviour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Verity | Yellow ball, most common| Normal |
| Skibity | Brown or Tanned ball. Its name is a mix of skibidi and verity.| Unknown |
| Scoobity | Light green or green ball| Unknown |
| Falsity | Vibrant blue ball.| Will be wrong on *almost* everything.|
