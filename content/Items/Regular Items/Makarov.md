**Makarov** was added 13th of November, 2022 as a gamepass.

**Makarov** itself could be used for 2 different things, combat and movement. Pretty self explanatary weapon, upon equipping player will lose his forcefield and be able to shoot other players, pushing himself opposite direction of the shot.

---
## Combat

**Makarov** has 8 ammo, deals 15 damage and 22.5 (1.5x) if player has hit head. Has high recoil, aiming lowers the recoil and slighty lowers users fov.

---
## Movement

Upon shooting, **Makarov** will generate force, pushing the player in the opposite direction that he shot. This can be used for numerous things such as: boosting yourself up to reach heights, pushing forward to jump across gaps, and if you shoot while seated, the force will push all attached parts to the seats, as long as they are not spiked or anchored

## Additional information

The Makarov is a gamepass item which is used for mobility or fighting
other players. It appears to be a small gun which is held in both hands
in front of the player.

## Usage

Upon use, the makarov fires a rather inaccurate but fast bullet towards
the cursor. Upon hit it deals some damage, more on headshot. Also knocks
in the opposite direction that their screen is facing, sometimes causing
[[Destabilization system|Destabilization]].
This can be used for faster movement, as well as being paired with the
[[Miracle of momentum|Miracle of momentum]] to fling
very far. It can fire 8 times on a magazine, but is fast to reload. Can
have the magazine swapped, even if full. Has no auto-reload like some
may be used to, it must be reloaded by manually pressing R.

## Obtainment

The Makarov can be purchased for 220 Robux.
