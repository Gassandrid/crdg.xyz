Premium Battery can be bought at [[Cart Dealership]] for 20 Robux. They're
used for opening the gate at the [[Cart Dealership|Cart Dealership]]. Once placed
into the slot, it will temporarily open the gate for longer than the [[Rusty Battery]], throwing the battery in to the void, although he battery can be saved by quickly taking it
out putting it behind a wall or spiking it down with a cable attached to the spike and the battery.
It has the alternate name of luxury battery when the seller at [[Cart Dealership]] says "robux for luxury battery".
