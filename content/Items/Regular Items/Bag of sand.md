## Usage

When holding this item and clicking, it will create 5 small sand parts. These sand parts can be heated to create glass, furnace in the hidden room of [[Places/Cathedral|Cathedral]], [[Flamethrower]] or campfire, but aside from this they have no special properties.

If one has the cardboard item, this can be used to create infinite debris to make cardboard with.

If the last person who has grabbed the sandbag died, and it has not yet respawned, the sandbag will not respawn again and will be gone from the server.
