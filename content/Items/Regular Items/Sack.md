## Overview

The sack is a brown sack, which can store 15 small objects for that specific server.

## Usage

The sack can store 15 unattached small objects, including [batteries](https://crdg.xyz/items/regular-items/rusty-battery).

Pressing E opens the inventory GUI, where you can see stored items, and can drag them out back into the world.
