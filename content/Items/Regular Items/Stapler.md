**Stapler** was added on 23rd of June, 2024. during the summer update pt1 alongside [[Cardboard]]. 

---
## Usage

**Stapler** is a great equivalent to [[content/Items/Regular Items/Glue|Glue]], as they serve the same purpose of welding together unanchored objects. A stapler's staples are smaller and thinner compared to those of [[content/Items/Regular Items/Glue|Glue]], letting the user leave less mess. Although it doesn't have keybinds, making working with stapler a bit more annoying.

## Additional information

## How to get the stapler {#how_to_get_the_stapler}

Get the [[Lemon|Lemon]] behind the Bill's Batteries,
then go to the Petrol Station, plant it and make a contraption that will
squeeze [[Lemon|lemons]]. Wait for it to grow (1.5)
hours

Cut the [[Lemon|lemons]] (you only need two lemons to
make a full glass) then squeeze the lemons over a glass. Drink the full
drink.
