**Marbles** were added on 3/12/2023, as a replacement of [[Flamethrower]] due to constant griefing and killing.

---

## Usage

Upon clicking, the player will drop 12 marbles onto the floor of various sizes. Each marble can trip a player, and will despawn after 80 seconds.

When the marble sack is emptied, the item changes into [Sack](https://crdg.xyz/items/regular-items/sack).
