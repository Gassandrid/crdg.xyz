 The Campfire is a Miscellaneous collectable that can be obtained via the  [[Features/Blimp|Blimp]].

## Additional information
The campfire releases dangerous carbon monoxide as of November 5, 2025 until now. When the carbon monoxide is breathed in it will cause you to slowly take damage until you die.

The carbon monoxide can be used to fill a balloon.

When standing on a campfire, you will start burning and losing body parts until death.

The campfire can be used as a light source.

The campfire can kill players even if they have a forcefield

## Function
The campfire's main function is to heat things up. It is incredibly useful for creating steam or gasoline as it acts like a portable ==heater==. All that is required is to pass a pipe above the campfire, when the pipe turns a hot ==yellow==.
