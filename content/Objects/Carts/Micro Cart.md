## Overview

The Micro Cart is a rare cart that has a chance of spawning at the Spawn Island cart spawners.

## Function

The race cart can increase its speed to an unlimited capacity, but will tend to glitch out at high speeds, much like the regular cart.

Can start and stop at the user's demand, with instant acceleration and braking.

Uses [diesel](https://crdg.xyz/features/liquids/diesel) as fuel and uses more at higher speeds.

Has a smaller frame compared to the regular cart.

The controls have been shuffled slightly, with the arrows being where the fuel meter and speedometer used to be

The micro cart has a faster, higher pitched horn than the regular cart.
