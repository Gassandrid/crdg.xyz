## Overview

The Race Cart is a rare cart that has a chance of spawning at the Spawn Island cart spawners.

## Function

The race cart can increase its speed to an unlimited capacity, in increments of 10, compared to the standard increment of 5, but will tend to glitch out at high speeds, much like the regular cart.

Can start and stop at the user's demand, with instant acceleration and braking.

Uses [diesel](https://crdg.xyz/features/liquids/diesel) as fuel and uses more at higher speeds.

Has a longer frame compared to the regular cart, and has a fuel tank guard that can be opened or closed by a button.

The speedometer and fuel meter are merged into the same two spots, and the horn can be played as long as its held.

The cart has headlights which can be enabled by turning on the horn.
