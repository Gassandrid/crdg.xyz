## Overview

The basic Cart is usually the first cart a player may ever have.

## Function

The cart can increase its speed to an unlimited capacity, but will tend to glitch out at high speeds.

Can start and stop at the user's demand, with instant acceleration and braking.

Uses [diesel](https://crdg.xyz/features/liquids/diesel) as fuel and uses more at higher speeds.
