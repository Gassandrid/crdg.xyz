The balloon stick, also known as the float stick, is a contraption made in the recent aerodynamics update using the trigger that has returned.

## Components

1x Skate

1x Trigger

1x  Balloon

1x Air compressor

1x Button

(8 component points)

Assembling

Attach the top/bottom of the skate to the top of the trigger, preferably on top of the handle and parallel to it. Next, grab the balloon and attach it to the top of the skate. Attach the string to the skate and use the air compressor powered by the button to fuel the balloon.

This contraption is single use, and is recommended to make many. It does not require makarov and u can simply walk in air.
