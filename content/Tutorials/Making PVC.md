*This page is about the process of creating PVC; for the page on the component, see [[PVC Board]].*

The [[Aerodynamics Update]] introduced the PVC board, an alternative to cardboard that can be produced automatically at the cost of creating a factory to produce it. This tutorial will guide you through the creation of PVC and the many steps required for it. You won't need to follow the same piping as the guide does if you know what you're doing.

---
## Materials

- Component Machine (2x points gamepass required)
- Glue (rubber glue not required)
- At least 4 water pumps (see [[Anvil]])
- At least 3 Campfires or Water Heaters (4 recommended)
- Various piping equipment made by the component machine
- 1 [[Smart Fluid Filter]]
- Many, many [[Fluid Entangloporter|fluid entangloporters]]/garden hoses of your choosing

---
## Getting Water

Among many others, [[Water]] is an essential resource required for the production of PVC. We will also need [[Salt Water]] later on, but the first step required is to create a renewable source of water. Position your 3 water pumps and stabilize them to a spot of your preference. We will need 3 separate sources; one for salt water and two for steam, so if you have multiple, connect some together.

> ![[WaterProducer.png]]
>
> An ideal water-producing setup. 6 water pumps are used here, but you can use 3.
## Steam

[[Steam]] is made from heating up [[Water]]. You can use a Water Heater for this, but a Campfire works too. Connect one of your water sources to the input of your heater of choosing and turn it on. If you get hot water, you can either use a sponge to void it or just keep it there.

> ![[SteamProducer.png]]
>
> Steam production. A campfire may be used for this.
## Salt Water

For this next step, we'll use a [[Mixing Tank|mixing tank]] recipe to get salt water. Position your mixing tank and attach a [[Split Pipe|split pipe]] to allow for two inputs to get into the tank at once. Connect one of the inputs to the gas output of the heater of your choice, and connect the second one to one of your water sources. Turn on the mixer, and salt water should successfully come out of the top output.

> ![[StillWaterProducer.png]] 
>
> Salt water production. 
## Chlorine

[[Chlorine]] is obtained by inputting [[Salt Water|salt water]] into a [[Liquid Electrolyzer]]. Connect the output of your mixing tank into the input of the electrolyzer, and turn it on. You should normally see [[Rye]] come out of the liquid pipe and chlorine come out of the gas pipe.

> ![[ChlorineProducer.png]]
>
> Chlorine production. Note that rye is not used in any parts of the guide, so you may use a sponge to void it.
## Ethylene

To complete the two-piece puzzle of [[Ethylene Dichloride|ethylene dichloride]], we must acquire [[Ethylene|ethylene]]. Fortunately, through the process of steam cracking, we are able to combine [[Petroleum|petroleum]] and [[Water|water]] to create ethylene. Merge four drills together into a square and connect them together to get a decent petroleum source. Next, use your third water source and turn it into steam with another heater of your choice and connect both outputs to the input of a mixing tank using a split pipe. Turn on the tank, and you should see ethylene coming out.

> ![[EthyleneProducer.png]]
>
> The current setup so far. 
## Ethylene Dichloride

For this next step, make a third mixing tank and simply connect the chlorine output of the electrolyzer and the ethylene output of the second tank to the input of your third tank. If you turn it on, ethylene dichloride should come out.
## Vinyl Chloride Monomer (VCM)

Heating up ethylene dichloride (*currently bugged and can only be heated up by campfires*) will turn it into VCM. It is recommended to use a smart fluid filter to filter out everything from that pipe (steam and carbon monoxide should not be with the VCM).

> ![[VCMProducer.png]]
>
>VCM production. 
## Finalizing

Once you get VCM, the only thing left is to heat it up and plug the output into a [[component factory]]. If you turn it on, you should get PVC, and the factory is complete. Feel free to make a few edits to this guide, and to customize your factory how you wish.
