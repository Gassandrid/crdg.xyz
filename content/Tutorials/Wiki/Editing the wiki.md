# Editing the wiki

You do not need GitHub, Git, or an account to fix a page.

1. Open the page you want to change.
2. Select **Edit this page** under its title.
3. Edit the text, or paste, drop, or select images to add them.
4. Use **Preview** to check your work. The **Markdown guide** shows the Obsidian syntax used by the
   wiki, including `[[wiki links]]`, `![[images.png]]`, callouts, tables, and lists.
5. Select **Review & submit**, briefly describe the change, and send it.

The edit is sent to the maintainers. It does not change the public wiki until a maintainer reviews
and approves it. The editor saves a private draft in your browser if you close it before submitting.

For large batches of pages, experienced contributors can still use the repository and pull-request
workflow.
