In CRDG, a fluid is a liquid or a gas. Liquids can be heated with a water cooler or boiled with a boiler from the gas station back room, the campfire, or a tiki torch. Gasses can be condensed with the gas condenser component

| Liquid | Description | Heated/Boiled |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Water | Water with no special properties. Found in the water cooler collectible, a non-respawning glass in the gas station cupboard room, and can be condensed from steam. | Hot water/Steam |
| Diesel | A fuel that can fill carts and pulsejets. Cannot fuel a gasoline engine because it's diesel, contrary to popular belief. Found in the diesel pump at the gas station. | Unknown/Diesel gas |
| Still water | Water that has been sitting still for too long. Can be sometimes found in the gas station cupboard room dripping from the ceiling or can be drilled from the spawn island. | Unknown/Steam |
